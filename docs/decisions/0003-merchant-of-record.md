# 0003 Merchant of record

Status: locked
Date: 2026-06

## Decision

TIDL is merchant of record, using PrescribeRx referred banking with vaulted
card infrastructure via Authorize.net or NMI.

## Rationale

High risk category. Deplatforming risk on peptides makes a general purpose
processor untenable.

## Consequences

TIDL carries chargeback exposure. Launch is one time purchase only,
subscription follows.
