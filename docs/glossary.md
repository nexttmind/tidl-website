# Glossary

Terms, partners, and systems. Keep short.

- PrescribeRx: clinical system of record. HIPAA, pharmacy routing, fulfillment,
  white labeled portals. Andrew, Nick.
- LocumTele: licensed provider network, medical directorship, LegitScript
  pathway. Joseph Palumbo.
- Small Patterns: design and frontend. Karim, Naz.
- Next Mind: prior frontend implementation against PrescribeRx APIs. Mohammad.
- Thia: AI infrastructure and orchestration for the clinical LLM. Elliot, Derek.
- Ask Tidl: on site educational assist (AskTidl.ai). Marketing and clinical
  modes. Spec: docs/specs/ask-tidl.md. UI talks only to `/api/ask-tidl`.
- Flow pen: the pre dosed auto injector. Love Lifesciences, UniPen.
- Clinical flow: intake → account → waiting → protocol (pay) or visit →
  confirmation. Spec: docs/specs/clinical-flow.md. Routes under `/care/*`.
